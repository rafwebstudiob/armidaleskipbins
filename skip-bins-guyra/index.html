Skip hire in Guyra

Guyra is a high-country town in the Armidale Regional Council area, surrounded by grazing land. Skip jobs here are often house clear-outs, renovations of older homes and shed or farm clean-ups. A 6–9 m³ skip suits most shed jobs. Because Guyra is about half an hour from Armidale, ask whether delivery distance affects the price.

Guyra Waste Transfer Station

The Guyra Waste Transfer Station and Community Recycling Centre is on Everett Street. Council lists it as open Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday, 9am to 5pm (no entry after 4:45pm). It's cashless, and sorted loads cost less than mixed loads. Council's fees say asbestos and bulk waste aren't accepted at Guyra, so a large clean-up suits a skip, and asbestos needs a licensed removalist and an approved facility.

Council rules

Guyra is in Armidale Regional Council. On your own land, a skip generally doesn't need approval. On a road or footpath, see the permit guide.
