Skip hire in Wollomombi

Wollomombi is a small village about 38 km east of Armidale, just north of Waterfall Way, at around 964 metres on the Northern Tablelands. It sits between the Wollomombi and Chandler rivers, which drop into Wollomombi Gorge at Wollomombi Falls in Oxley Wild Rivers National Park. The land around it is mostly grazing country, so skip jobs are often farm and shed clean-ups, old fencing and iron, and house clear-outs. A 6–9 m³ skip suits most shed jobs.

Distance and access

Wollomombi is about 42 km from Armidale by road, so ask whether delivery distance affects the price, and allow some flexibility on the delivery day. Mention long driveways, gates, cattle grids or soft ground in the form, and choose a firm, level spot the truck can reach.

Nearest tip

The closest council facility is Armidale Regional Council's Armidale Waste Management Facility at 108 Long Swamp Road, Armidale. Council lists it as open Monday to Friday 7:30am to 5pm and weekends and public holidays 12pm to 5pm (no entry after 4:45pm). It's closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day, Good Friday, Easter Sunday, New Year's Day and Anzac Day. Payment is card only, no cash, and mixed loads that aren't sorted are charged a higher fee. From Wollomombi that's a long round trip, so a skip often makes more sense for a big clean-up.

Council rules

Wollomombi is in Armidale Regional Council. On your own land, a skip generally doesn't need approval. On a road or road reserve, see the permit guide.
