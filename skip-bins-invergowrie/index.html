Skip hire in Invergowrie

Invergowrie is a rural-residential locality about 16 km west of Armidale, popular with University of New England staff. It has a general store, a fuel station and a Rural Fire Service station. Most homes are on lifestyle blocks, so skip jobs are often shed clean-outs, renovation waste, green waste and moving clear-outs. A 4 m³ skip suits a bathroom, 6 m³ a kitchen, and 6–9 m³ most shed or block clean-ups.

A different council

Invergowrie is in Uralla Shire Council, not Armidale Regional Council, so our Armidale permit guide doesn't apply here. If the skip needs to go on the road or road reserve, ask Uralla Shire Council first. A skip wholly on your own land is the simplest option. On a bigger block, tell the operator about gates, gravel driveways and soft ground.

Nearest tip

Uralla Shire Council's Uralla Waste Management Facility and Community Recycling Centre is on Tip Road, off Rowan Avenue, Uralla. Council lists it as open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 8am to 4pm and Saturday and Sunday 9am to 2pm, closed Wednesdays, Fridays, Christmas Day, Good Friday and Anzac Day, with no commercial loads on weekends. It takes asbestos if you book at least 24 hours ahead. Fees apply to some items, including building waste and general household waste. For a big clean-out, a skip saves several trips.

Get the waste type right

Older farmhouses and sheds can contain fibro, which may contain asbestos and can't go in a general skip. Choose "May contain asbestos" in the form if you're unsure. See what can go in a skip.
